I build things. Most of them start with a problem I can't stop thinking about.

          
First year Electrical Engineering student in the International Undergraduate Program at ITS. My curiosity sits between hardware and AI, and those two keep running into each other.

          
Before university I spent high school doing research. I worked on EcoMeth, a home biogas system that turns organic household waste into cooking fuel, and FLOBRIC Solar Still, a low cost water distiller for communities without reliable clean water. Neither won. Both taught me how to take a messy real world problem, research it properly, and defend my reasoning in front of judges.

          
At ITS I reached the semifinals of the National Olympiad ELECTRA twice, in 2024 and 2025, both hosted by my own department. That is where I knew electrical engineering was the right place for me.

          
Then I started building for real. An offline MP3 player with a custom PCB I designed and had fabricated. An agentic AI assistant running on Google's Gemini API. I hold AI certifications from OpenAI Academy and a digital marketing certification from Google Skillshop.

          
Sharing this is the part I enjoy most. I regularly pull friends into study and skill building sessions, because learning alone gets boring fast.

HARDWARE

        
Offline MP3 Player

        
Built a portable music player from scratch. ESP32-S3 microcontroller, PCM5102 DAC, custom NE5532 amplifier circuit, 1.3 inch OLED display, and microSD storage for fully offline playback.

        
Designed the PCB in KiCad and had it fabricated. Wrote the firmware in Arduino IDE. Used Gemini to debug driver and signal issues I would have lost days to.

AI

        
Agentic AI Assistant

        
Built and run a personal agentic assistant that handles real work: long-term memory, tool use, scheduled automations, and multi-step task execution across terminal, browser, Google Workspace and GitHub.

        
It routes every model call through 9Router, an OpenAI-compatible gateway that unifies 41 models across 5 providers (Claude, DeepSeek, MiniMax, GLM, Qwen, Gemini and more) behind one endpoint, so it can pick the best model per task and fail over between providers. Started on the Google Gemini API, then grew into a full multi-provider agent.

        
Developed 21 custom skills with it along the way: second-brain notes, portfolio and LinkedIn management, KiCad PCB workflows, classroom monitoring and recurring briefings.
